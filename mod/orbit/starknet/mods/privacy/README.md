# starknet.privacy — Privacy

The STRK20 shielded pool: what it holds, its live activity, and the helpers that act on shielded notes. Read-only, no keys.

An app on Starknet, listed in the starknet **Apps** tab. Its UI is the `/privacy` page of the starknet app (`app/app/privacy/page.tsx`).

- address: `/starknet/apps/privacy/`
- fns: `facts`, `pool`, `activity`, `user`, `helpers`, `docs`  ->  `GET /starknet/_api/apps/privacy/<fn>`  or  `m starknet.privacy/<fn>`
- `facts()` is what the Apps card shows.
