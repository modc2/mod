# starknet.contracts — Contracts

Your Cairo contracts with their devnet address and build state, plus the well-known mainnet contracts to read by ABI.

An app on Starknet, listed in the starknet **Apps** tab. Its UI is the `/contracts` page of the starknet app (`app/app/contracts/page.tsx`).

- address: `/starknet/apps/contracts/`
- fns: `facts`, `contracts`  ->  `GET /starknet/_api/apps/contracts/<fn>`  or  `m starknet.contracts/<fn>`
- `facts()` is what the Apps card shows.
