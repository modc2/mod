Miners and validators who never learn who the other is.

Each one only knows that the other is in the registered set. Every submit, score and claim is a ring signature over the STARK curve, and a Cairo contract verifies it on-chain. A validator scores a tag, never a miner. A miner gets paid and never learns which validators scored it.

01 · MINER

submit

The miner signs work with a ring of miners in scope H(mine, subnet, epoch). The key image is its tag for this epoch. That gives one submission per miner per epoch, and the tag can't be linked to its key or to its tags in other epochs.

02 · VALIDATOR

score

The validator signs a 0-100 score with a ring of validators in scope H(score, subnet, epoch, tag). The key image is a nullifier. That gives one score per validator per tag, and its scores on different tags can't be linked.

03 · MINER

claim

After the epoch closes, the miner re-signs in the same mine scope. The same key gives the same image, which proves it owns the tag. It credits emission x sum / total to any recipient, and a replay is rejected.

READ THIS · THE SET-SIZE LIMIT

  • Your anonymity set is the ring, nothing more. With 3 validators each score points to 1 of 3. The contract enforces min_ring and rejects padded rings (key ids strictly increasing).
  • Cost is linear in the ring. A ring of 4 is about 16k Cairo steps and 19 ec_op. The cap is 64.
  • The tx sender is public. Actions go through a relayer, because the contract never checks the caller. Timing, IP and work content can still identify you.
  • Payouts are public books. Points are credited in storage, and no token moves.
