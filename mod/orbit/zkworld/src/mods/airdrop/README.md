# airdrop

Airdrop: claim your allocation with a Poseidon Merkle proof.

A Cairo contract mod (submod of `zkworld`), addressed `zkworld.airdrop`. A self-contained Scarb project: `src/lib.cairo` is the contract, `tests/` the snforge tests.

```sh
m zkworld.airdrop/build
m zkworld.airdrop/test
m zkworld.airdrop/vibe prompt="..."
```
