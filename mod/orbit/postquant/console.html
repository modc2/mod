what this is

A post-quantum L1 whose entire state machine is a market in key/value space. There is one thing to own — a key in the store — and the whole protocol is the market for it.

signaturesML-DSA (FIPS 204, lattice). A signature is 2420 bytes and the chain bills for every one of them. commitmentsSHA3-256 throughout — addresses, state root, Merkle proofs. Grover halves the exponent; 256 bits stays 128. no curvesNothing here is ed25519 or secp256k1, because a curve is exactly the thing Shor's algorithm takes apart. write gasOne-time, per byte. A key byte costs 4x a value byte — a key is an index entry the whole network sorts forever. A value declared as a hash is cheaper still: the chain prices commitments below blobs on purpose. witness gasPer byte of signature and public key. A chain that charges a flat 21000 for a transfer quietly subsidises its own witnesses; this one does not. rentPer byte per hour against a prepaid escrow. When it runs dry the entry expires and anyone may sweep it for the bond the writer put up — which is what makes expiry real rather than advisory. Delete the entry yourself and the bond comes back whole. fee marketEIP-1559 over state growth instead of execution: if the store grows faster than target, writing gets more expensive until it stops. Base fee burned, tip to the proposer. the marketList a key you own at a price; a buyer takes it with its remaining lease. Human-readable keys are the point — you cannot bid on something you cannot say. consensusSingle proposer — authority, not agreement. What is real is the ordered log, deterministic execution, and a state root anyone can recompute: VERIFY THE CHAIN replays every block from genesis and re-checks every root. apiSame functions three ways: REST under , MCP (22 tools) at POST /mcp, and this console.
