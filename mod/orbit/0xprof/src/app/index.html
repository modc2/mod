Paste or drop a proof. Every method that can check it will, each one on its own, and you get every answer — not a summary. Nothing is stored and no account is needed.

A market needs supply. These four need no trusted setup, so they are cheap enough to prove right here — the secret never leaves the request, and the proof is checked by two independent verifiers before you see it. The snark systems need a proving key; bring one to the API.
