Kensington Market

Walk or skate Augusta, Baldwin and Kensington Avenue — a hundred and fifty Victorian houses painted every colour there is, fruit stacked to head height in the doorways, vintage on rails out under the awnings, murals on every blank wall and the whole sky cut into pieces by the hydro wires. Drums going in Bellevue Square, a busker on the corner, the car full of plants still parked where somebody left it in 1994, and the CN Tower over the rooftops. Sunrise to midnight on one key. Walk up to anyone and press F — they've all got a name, a job and an opinion, and if the conversation goes well enough you'll leave with a number. Nine people down here want a favour. WebGL, no install, sound and all.
