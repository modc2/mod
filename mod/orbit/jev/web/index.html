Loading the price…
Each question is paid as you ask it: the server answers 402, your wallet signs a USDC transfer for exactly the price (an EIP-3009 authorization — no gas, nothing moves until the facilitator settles it), and the question runs. Nothing is stored about you but the on-chain transfer.
Prepay once, then each question costs one signature and no transaction. Credits belong to the paying address; spending them needs a sign-in (a signed token) for that address.
