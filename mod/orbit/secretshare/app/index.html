secretshare

Split a password, seed phrase or key into pieces. Hand them to people you trust. Any few of them together can get it back, and no single piece reveals anything.

  1. 1. SplitType the secret and choose how many pieces, and how many are needed to unlock it.
  2. 2. Hand outGive each person one piece. Keep them apart. Throw away the original if you want.
  3. 3. UnlockWhen it's needed, paste in enough pieces and the secret comes back.

Split a secret

Everything happens in this browser tab. Nothing is sent to any server.

Unlock a secret

Paste the pieces you have, one per line. Order doesn't matter.

Inspect a piece

Paste one share to see its set ID, threshold, and index — without revealing the secret.

Shamir's Secret Sharing over GF(256), with a checksum so wrong pieces fail loudly instead of returning garbage. Pieces also work from the command line: m secretshare/split "…" n=5 k=3 · m secretshare/combine "ss1… ss1… ss1…".
