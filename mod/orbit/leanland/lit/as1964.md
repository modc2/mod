## What I actually want from it

- 26.2.1: the normal density.
- 26.2.17: Zelen & Severo's rational approximation to the normal CDF, absolute
  error < 7.5e-8.

## Why the approximation rather than a library erf

Parity. `math.erf`, Rust's and a JS polyfill are three different algorithms;
lowering 26.2.17 from one definition gives one algorithm in three places, and
the error bound is stated rather than assumed.
