Washington Square Park

Walk or skate the Fifth Avenue promenade, the fountain plaza and the tree-lined walks. Drum circles going, buskers pulling a crowd, chess hustlers in the south-west corner, half the park sat on the fountain rim, pigeons going up as you run through them. Sunrise to midnight on one key. Walk up to anyone and press F — they've all got a name, a job and an opinion, and if the conversation goes well enough you'll leave with a number. Nine people in the park want a favour. WebGL, no install, sound and all.
